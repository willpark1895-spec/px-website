How It Works

One address. Four steps. Real numbers.

TerraValue connects to live public ArcGIS parcel databases across 10 Georgia jurisdictions. It pulls your lot size, assessed value, and canopy data — then runs every number through peer-reviewed USDA and EPA methodologies.

The result: a complete picture of what your land's ecosystem is worth in actual dollars, broken down across six services, with a branded PDF report you can take to a meeting.

Enter any address

Nominatim geocodes it. We find the parcel in real time.

Auto-fill parcel data

Cascading ArcGIS lookup: city endpoint first, county fallback. Lot size, assessed value, canopy %.

3

Calculate everything

Six ecosystem services, dual GI methodology (EPA + GA Blue Book), tree inventory, and land valuation.

4

Results + report

Interactive dashboard with satellite map, charts, per-service breakdown, and downloadable PDF.

What We Measure

Six ecosystem services, quantified

Every value is calculated from peer-reviewed research. Every formula traceable to a source paper.

$494/yr

Carbon Sequestration

Trees absorb CO2 and store it as biomass. We calculate annual sequestration per canopy acre and price it at the EPA social cost of carbon.

2.6 t CO2/acre/yr (Atlanta iTree Eco) × $190/tonne (EPA SC-GHG 2023, 2% near-term)

$520/yr

Stormwater Management

Canopy intercepts 35% of rainfall. We calculate gallons diverted from the storm system and price at municipal avoided treatment costs.

Benefit transfer from USDA CUFR / iTree Eco urban canopy valuation literature

$418/yr

Air Quality Improvement

Trees filter PM2.5, ozone, NO2, and SO2. Atlanta's PM2.5 concentration makes this one of the highest-value services.

Nowak et al. 2014, BenMAP-CE health valuation ($117K/ton PM2.5 national median)

$252/yr

Energy Savings

Strategic canopy reduces cooling loads in summer and wind exposure in winter. Avoided kWh per canopy acre.

McPherson 2003, Atlanta iTree Eco: 1,800 kWh/acre/yr × $0.14/kWh (GA Power avg)

$320/yr

Habitat Value

Canopy provides nesting, foraging, and corridor habitat. Priced using willingness-to-pay studies for urban biodiversity.

Troy & Wilson 2006; Brander & Koetse 2011 (benefit transfer, approximate)

+7%

Property Value Premium

Mature canopy coverage adds a measurable premium to assessed property values. Often the single largest dollar amount.

Kovacs et al. 2022; Netusil et al. 2014: ~7% premium (capped at 12%)
