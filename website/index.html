the soil principle

most people look at the canopy. we start underground.

Everything visible above ground — every branch, every leaf, every dollar of ecosystem value — depends entirely on what's happening below. If the system underneath is out of balance, no amount of above-ground intervention will hold.

diagnose, don't guess

Full ecosystem assessment before any prescription. Soil, roots, hydrology, structure — the whole system.

work with nature, not against it

Regenerative, nature-based solutions. Things that get stronger over time, not weaker.
