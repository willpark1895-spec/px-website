Our Approach

We Don't Treat Symptoms. We Find the Root Cause.

Soil health is everything — it dictates structure, resilience, longevity, and every visible symptom a client calls about. If the system below is out of balance, no amount of above-ground intervention will hold.

  • Holistic, systems-level diagnostic approach — examining the full ecosystem before prescribing solutions
  • Nature-based, regenerative solutions that work with natural systems rather than against them
  • Public-sector credibility combined with private-sector responsiveness and personal attention
  • Recommendations that last longer, cost less over time, and address actual causes rather than surface symptoms

Services

Comprehensive Consulting, Expert Execution

Our principal consultant serves as the diagnostic expert, designer, and project lead on every engagement. Specialized field work is executed through a curated network of trusted subcontractors.

Landscape Diagnostics

Comprehensive site assessments that start below the surface. Soil testing, root-zone evaluation, hydrology analysis, and ecosystem health mapping to identify true drivers of decline.

Urban Tree Consulting

Risk assessment, preservation planning, inventory and GIS mapping, species selection, planting design, and long-term canopy management strategies for any property type.

Regenerative Design

Nature-based design solutions: native plantings, pollinator habitat, rain gardens, bioswales, soil remediation, and integrated stormwater management. Beautiful, functional, self-sustaining.

Construction & Development

Tree preservation plans, root protection zones, grading impact assessments, and on-site monitoring. Helping developers meet ordinance requirements while protecting natural assets.

Soil Health & Remediation

Soil compaction mitigation, biological inoculant programs, organic amendment plans, drainage redesign, and long-term soil restoration strategies. The foundation of every healthy landscape.

Ordinance & Grant Support

Tree ordinance compliance, permit consulting, and grant writing for USDA, EPA, and state-level funding. We speak the same language as the municipal reviewers — because we are one.
