What We Measure

Six Ecosystem Services, Quantified

Every calculation is grounded in published, peer-reviewed research from the USDA Forest Service, EPA, and leading environmental science journals.

Carbon Sequestration

Annual CO₂ captured by your canopy, valued at the EPA's social cost of carbon: $255 per metric tonne. Southeast Piedmont rate: 2.6 tonnes CO₂/acre/year.

Atlanta iTree Eco 2014-2015; Rennert et al. 2022, Nature

Stormwater Management

Rainfall intercepted by canopy before it becomes runoff. Valued at municipal avoided-cost rates: $4.00 per 1,000 gallons at 35% canopy interception.

USDA Forest Service CUFR Fact Sheet #4

Air Quality Improvement

Pollution removed from the atmosphere — ozone, PM2.5, NO₂, SO₂, CO. Weighted by health impact: $28.50/lb for the Southeast, PM2.5-heavy.

Nowak et al. 2014; EPA BenMAP

Energy Savings

Reduced electricity consumption from canopy shading and windbreak effects. Southeast rate: 1,800 kWh/acre/year at local utility rates.

McPherson 2003; Atlanta iTree Eco

Habitat & Biodiversity

Ecological value of urban forest habitat for wildlife, pollinators, and native species. Valued at $320 per acre of canopy using willingness-to-pay studies.

Troy & Wilson 2006, Ecological Economics

Property Value Premium

Well-canopied properties carry a 7% premium in assessed value. This meta-analysis finding holds across property types and US regions.

Kovacs et al. 2022, USDA NRS meta-analysis
